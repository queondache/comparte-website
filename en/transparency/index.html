Privacy notice

Who processes your data

Comparte Onlus, Via G. G. Porro 8, 00197 Rome, tax code 97977810585. Contact: info@comparte.it.

What data we collect

Your email address and, if you enter it, your name.

Why

Only to send you the Comparte newsletter with updates on projects, activities and fundraising initiatives. The legal basis is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR).

Who manages it

The newsletter is sent through Infomaniak Network SA, a provider based in Switzerland, where the data is stored. The European Commission recognises Switzerland as providing an adequate level of protection. We do not sell or pass your data to anyone.

Statistics

We use Infomaniak tracking features to record email opens and clicks and understand which content interests you most. Tracking uses detection images and dedicated links. It may collect data associated with the recipient, such as the time of opening, IP address, approximate location and the type of device or email software used. This data may also be processed in aggregate reports.

How long we keep it

Until you unsubscribe.

Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your data, or withdraw your consent at any time: click the unsubscribe link at the end of any email or write to info@comparte.it. You can also lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority.
